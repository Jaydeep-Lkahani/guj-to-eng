# Video review

Videos waiting for your review. Rules are in `pending.md` section 2 (V.1 to V.5).

**How it works**
1. The builder finds 2–5 candidate videos for a topic and confirms each one exists with YouTube oEmbed (HTTP 200). The title and channel below are copied exactly from that answer.
2. Each candidate is added to `content/videos.json` with `status: "candidate"` and listed in the table below.
3. You watch the video and check it against the quality list (V.2). Then tell the builder in chat: **approve** or **reject** (with a reason).
4. Only then does the builder set the status to `approved` or `rejected`. Only approved videos appear on the website.

Nobody may invent, guess or remember a YouTube ID. Only IDs confirmed by oEmbed are written here.

## Quality checklist (V.2)
- Clear, simple, everyday Gujarati (not heavy textbook Gujarati, not mostly Hindi or English)
- The tutor's English examples are grammatically correct
- The tutor's pronunciation avoids the common mistakes in `pending.md` section 1 (for example "ઇસ્કૂલ", V/W mix-up, "જૂ" for zoo)
- Teaches step by step, with a board or on-screen text, many examples and Gujarati comparisons
- Right level for the topic and phase
- Clear audio and video; text readable on a phone screen
- On topic only; no long intro, selling or off-topic talk
- Family-friendly and free to watch
- The link still works (all links are re-checked every 3 months)
